01 — The challenge

Connect the presentation of internet services with their application process: from browsing plans and checking coverage to following up on a request. The platform combines a public experience for prospective customers with internal tools for managing the process.

02 — My participation

I participated in building the project from scratch as part of a team. The work involved creating a platform that connects the public experience with internal service application processes. The capabilities below describe the product developed by the team.

The team followed the Scrum agile methodology, organizing development into sprints. We used Git and GitLab for version control and Trello to organize tasks and track progress.

03 — The platform

  • Public website with residential and business offerings, plans, and frequently asked questions.
  • Coverage checks based on the prospective customer’s location.
  • Online application and preliminary contract workflow.
  • Internal request management, document review, corrections, and finalization.

Laravel and Vue.js connect through Inertia.js. The geographic functionality uses PostgreSQL, PostGIS, and Leaflet to relate a prospective customer’s location to coverage zones.

04 — The technical approach

The project brings together an approachable interface, backend validation, workflow states, and access control for internal tools. Geographic management also requires validating coverage data before using it in a query.
